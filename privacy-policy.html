Privacy Policy — Scatter & Grace Pin Publisher

Effective date: October 23, 2026
Last updated: October 23, 2026

Who we are

Scatter & Grace Pin Publisher ("the App") is a private software tool owned and operated by Michael Stoffers, doing business as Scatter & Grace ("we," "us," "the operator"), a small art and printable-products brand based in Wisconsin, USA. The App is an internal tool used only by the operator himself. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can register for, log into, or use it.

Questions about this policy or the App's data practices can be sent to assistantaugustine@gmail.com.

What the App does

The App connects to the operator's own Pinterest business account through Pinterest's official API, using OAuth 2.0 authorization granted by the operator. It reads the operator's own board list so Pins are filed correctly, and it creates image Pins — product images, titles, descriptions, and links — for the operator's own products on the operator's own boards, on a schedule the operator sets. The products are the operator's own designs sold through Redbubble and Zazzle.

Information the App accesses

The App accesses only the operator's own Pinterest account data, specifically:

  • Account identity information returned by the API (such as username and account ID), used solely to confirm the App is operating on the correct account.
  • Board information for the operator's own boards (such as board names and IDs), used solely to file Pins to the correct board.
  • Pin information for Pins the App itself created, used solely to verify successful publication.

The App does not access, collect, or store information about any other Pinterest user. It has no other users.

Pin content

Pin content (images, titles, descriptions, and destination links) is created and supplied by the operator and consists of the operator's own product material. No personal information about any third party is included in Pin content.

OAuth tokens and storage

When the operator authorizes the App, Pinterest issues an access token and refresh token. These tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer, are used only to authenticate the App's requests to Pinterest's API on the operator's behalf, and are never sent to any third party, server, or service other than Pinterest's own API endpoints. The App does not maintain a database of Pinterest data; it requests current information from the API when needed.

Information sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, share, or disclose any information accessed through the Pinterest API to any third party, for any purpose, including advertising or analytics.

Cookies and tracking

The App does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, analytics trackers, or any third-party tracking technology.

Data retention and deletion

  • OAuth tokens are retained only while the App is in use. The operator can revoke the App's access at any time through Pinterest's account settings (Apps / authorized apps), which immediately invalidates the tokens.
  • Because the App stores no database of Pinterest data, there is no stored personal data set to delete. Any locally stored token or configuration file is deleted on request, and in any event when the App is retired.
  • To request deletion of any information associated with the App, or to ask any question about this policy, contact assistantaugustine@gmail.com. Requests will be honored within 30 days.

Children's privacy

The App is an internal business tool, is not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes will not expand the App's data practices beyond what is described here without an updated policy being published first.
